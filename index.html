I am a Research Fellow in the Center for Computational Mathematics at the Flatiron Institute. I focus on deep generative models and machine learning in the sciences. I'm also an ai safety fellow with the interpretability team at Anthropic.

My ongoing work is a mix of theory and methods for diffusion- and flow-based models and applications to chemistry and to nuclear fusion energy ⚛️. See here for previous work on flow maps, more flow maps, models for fluids/PDEs, design choices for flows, coupling base and target densities, time-varying causality, cardiogenic shock, drug-induced ECG abnormalities, nonlinear diffusion, multimodal representation learning, diffusion noise schedules, and understanding OOD tests.


Previously, I completed my PhD at NYU's Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences, advised by Rajesh Ranganath. I also worked closely with Eric Vanden-Eijnden. Before that, I studied music composition.
